This notice describes the current MakeIn preview app and this marketing website. MakeIn is a dating app for adults aged 18 and over.

What the app uses

Google sign-in provides an account identifier and account information, such as your name, email and Google photo. Onboarding asks for your birth date to check adult eligibility, plus the dating profile details you choose to share: first name, gender, who you want to meet, city, relationship intentions, interests, bio and photos. Discovery preferences help filter profiles.

The app also processes likes, passes, mutual matches, messages, profile changes, blocks and reports so its features and safety controls can work. Account and service records may be needed to diagnose failures and protect the service.

What other people can see

Your public dating profile contains the information you publish, including your age and profile photos. Your birth date and email are stored as private account information, not public profile fields. Conversation participants can see messages in their shared conversation. A mutual match is required to start a new conversation.

Only add photos and information that you have permission to share. Another person may save information you show or send them, so choose what to share thoughtfully.

Where information is handled

MakeIn uses Google Firebase for authentication and app data. Firestore is configured in the Asia South region (Mumbai); this is not a promise that every supporting Google service processes information only in that location. Firebase security rules restrict access by account and feature.

Photo upload depends on Firebase Storage provisioning and may be unavailable in the preview. An existing Google photo or another persistent HTTPS photo URL may be used instead. Images loaded from a photo URL are handled by that image host.

Demo profiles and conversations

Demo profiles are fictional. Demo likes, matches and practice messages stay in the current session and are separate from real member interactions. The profiles and conversation on this website are also illustrations.

Payments

Payments and automatic renewal are not enabled in this preview. There is no live checkout or payment mandate. The app includes a planned membership design, but only a verified entitlement could unlock paid real interactions. Any future payment collection will need updated purchase disclosures and provider information before checkout.

Your controls and account deletion

You can edit your profile and use blocking, reporting and unmatching controls in the app. Account deletion includes cleanup of your profile, likes, matches and conversations. If deletion is interrupted, the app allows you to retry. Until cleanup finishes, some account data may remain.

Deleting an account cannot remove copies another person has saved or all records independently held by external providers. A fixed backup or service-log deletion schedule has not been published for the preview.

This website

This static website has no account form, email collection form or advertising analytics script. Its fonts are hosted with the page. The illustrative portrait is loaded from Unsplash, which receives the information needed to serve that image, such as your IP address. The website host may process ordinary request logs.

Following a preview link opens an external service, such as Expo, with its own privacy practices. The mobile app’s data handling is separate from browsing this website.

Changes and questions

This notice will change as the preview develops. Review the date above when returning. For an existing account, use the app’s profile and deletion controls to manage your information. Safety concerns can be reported from the relevant profile or conversation.

A dedicated external privacy contact has not been published yet. This preview notice does not promise a support channel that is not currently available.
