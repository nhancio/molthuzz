LET’S CLEAR A FEW THINGS UP

Good questions.

Who is MakeIn for?+

MakeIn is a dating app for adults aged 18 and over. Share your interests and relationship intentions, set your preferences, and discover people you’d like to get to know.

Can I use MakeIn in my browser?+

MakeIn is built for Android and iOS. This website introduces the mobile app and links to current preview availability. Dating, profiles and conversations happen inside the app.

Is the app available in the stores?+

There is no announced Google Play or App Store release. The Android link opens the Expo preview build page, where its actual build status and any available download are shown. iOS preview availability is listed below.

Are the profiles on this page real?+

No. The screens here are design illustrations, with fictional profiles and conversations. App demo profiles are also fictional; demo likes, matches and practice messages stay in your current session.

What can I try before payments launch?+

Profile setup and the clearly labelled demo are free. Paid real interactions stay locked because payment processing and membership activation are not enabled. Existing history and safety controls remain available.

Can I delete my account?+

Yes. The app includes account deletion with cleanup of your profile, likes, matches and conversations. If a step is interrupted, you can retry it. Read more about the data involved in our privacy notice.
