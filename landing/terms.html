These are the current conditions for the MakeIn preview. MakeIn is a mobile dating app, and this website explains the app and its availability.

Adults only, and always yourself

You must be at least 18 to use MakeIn. Provide an accurate birth date and profile information, use your own account, and share only content and photos you have the right to use. Do not impersonate someone or misrepresent your identity.

Respect the person on the other side

Do not harass, threaten, exploit, discriminate against or scam other people. Do not share illegal content, someone else’s private information or intimate content without consent. Respect a pass, an unmatch and a block. Do not evade safety controls or access another person’s account.

Use the app’s reporting and blocking controls when needed. A report does not promise an immediate response or a particular outcome. The preview does not provide an emergency service.

Connections and safety

Likes and mutual matches help people choose who to meet. They do not verify a person’s identity, background, intentions or safety. MakeIn does not promise a match, a reply or a relationship. Use your judgment when sharing personal information, communicating or arranging to meet.

What “preview” means

The app is under development. Features can change, fail or be temporarily unavailable. The Android preview link shows the actual cloud build status and any available download. iOS availability is described on the homepage. Neither an Expo project nor a preview build means that MakeIn is published in Google Play or the App Store.

Demo people and messages are fictional. Demo likes, matches and practice conversations are kept in the current session. Illustrations on this site are examples, not real members or evidence of activity.

Planned membership, with payments disabled

The planned launch price is ₹2 for the first calendar month, then ₹99 per month in INR, with automatic monthly renewal once payments are launched. The first calendar month is not a 30-day trial. No checkout, mandate or charge is currently enabled, and these displayed prices do not start a subscription.

Profile setup and the session-only demo are free. Real likes and new chats that require membership remain locked until an active entitlement can be verified. Existing history and safety controls remain accessible when membership expires.

Before any live purchase, the app must show the actual charge, applicable period, renewal terms and cancellation control. The planned cancellation behavior is to stop future renewal while preserving already paid access through its end. Payment processing remains deferred; no paid membership is offered by this website.

Your account and information

You can edit your profile and use account deletion in the app. Cleanup can be retried if interrupted. Please read the privacy notice to understand public profile fields, private account information, messages, providers and current preview limitations.

Availability and changes

Preview access and these conditions may change as MakeIn develops. These terms do not remove rights you have under applicable law. No external customer-support or legal-contact address has been published yet; available account and safety controls are in the app.
