CHILD SAFETY STANDARDS

Zero tolerance for
child exploitation.

MakeIn is an 18+ app operated by Nhancio. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.

Adults only

Every account must confirm a birth date showing the user is 18 or older. The age check is enforced in the app and again on our servers. Accounts we believe belong to minors are removed.

What is prohibited

Any content, message, photo, profile or behaviour that sexualises minors, grooms or solicits minors, shares or requests CSAM, or facilitates trafficking or exploitation of children. Violations lead to immediate account removal and a permanent ban.

How to report

Use Report on any profile or conversation inside MakeIn, or email hello@nhancio.com with the subject “Child safety report”. Reports about child safety are reviewed with the highest priority.

How we respond

We review reports promptly, remove violating content and accounts, preserve relevant information as required by law, and report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant national and regional authorities, including Indian law enforcement under applicable law.

Child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for CSAE and CSAM prevention and compliance is Nithin Didigam, reachable at nithindidigam@nhancio.com.
