ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your
MakeIn account.

You can delete your MakeIn account and associated data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

Open MakeIn, go to Profile → Account → Delete account, confirm by signing in again and typing DELETE. Deletion starts immediately.

Without the app

Email hello@nhancio.com from the email address linked to your Google or Apple sign-in, with the subject “MakeIn account deletion request”. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, profile (name, birth date, gender, bio, interests, prompts, city), profile photos, likes, friend requests, matches, conversations, call records, blocks and the reports you filed.

What may be kept

Reports other members filed about your account may be kept for up to 12 months for safety and legal compliance. Payment records, if any, are kept as required by law. See the privacy policy for details.
